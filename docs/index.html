Before your first note

A few good
questions.

Ask us something else →
Do I need experience?+

You can start as a beginner, return after time away, or continue with experience. Tell us about your goals so we can help you choose the right next step.

Where can I take lessons?+

Explore studio lessons in New Braunfels and Helotes. In-home lessons are also available in the San Antonio area, subject to teacher and location availability.

How long are private lessons?+

The school offers weekly 30-, 45-, and 60-minute private lesson options. Ask which option fits your instrument, goals, and schedule.

How do I book a trial?+

Choose your preferred studio on our trial page, then view its available lesson times. Check the instrument, teacher, price, and booking details before choosing a time.
