What to review

  1. Look and feel

    The original FM blue, black, white, and gray palette, logo, photography, typography, and overall personality of the school. Review the Instagram and Facebook displays on the homepage, too.

  2. Lessons and people

    Instrument descriptions, instructor names and roles, and lesson formats. Tell us any age requirements or availability details that should be added.

  3. Locations and booking

    Addresses, trial booking paths, in-home service areas, and the listed travel fee. Confirm any pricing details before publication.

  4. Your visitor’s experience

    Try the menu, instrument pages, location pages, and phone links on mobile.
