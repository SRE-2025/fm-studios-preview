Start with instrument fit

Instrument fit matters. Ask about sizing and setup before buying or renting a violin, and tell us about any music you already play.

A violin that fits the player is an important starting point. Ask the school about sizing, bow condition and a comfortable support setup. Bring school-orchestra music if you would like to discuss it.
